The order book nobody stands on

Markets desk

Every fifteen minutes a new question opens on DreamDEX and closes again, and most of them go to settlement without a single trade against them. The pricing is not the problem. The problem is that the person with an opinion and the venue where that opinion pays out are never in the same room.

That gap is the whole business of this newsletter. Eleven thousand people read this page every week and argue about exactly one thing: which way the next candle goes. Until now the only thing we could sell them was an ad.

A reader who disagrees with us in the comments is worth nothing. A reader who disagrees with us on the book is worth twenty five basis points.

So here is the market, in the page, in the middle of the argument. It is a real Event Contract on Somnia Shannon. The odds below are read from the live order book, the trade is signed by the reader's own wallet, and this newsletter earns a share of every order its readers route through it.

Take the other side

Nothing about this page was built for it. There is no bundler here, no framework, no shared stylesheet with the widget, and no server of our own. The file you are reading is static HTML on disk, which is the point: if it works here it works in a Ghost post, a Webflow block, a Discord landing page or a docs site.

The reader keeps custody the whole way through. Kiosk never touches the position, only the referral, and the split is enforced on chain by a contract we do not control either.

Shannon Weekly is a fictional publication built to exercise a real integration. The market, the order book, the wallet prompt and the fee split are all live on Somnia Shannon testnet (chain 50312). This file is static HTML on disk loading kiosk-dreamdex.vercel.app/embed.js, so it runs from anywhere.
